# Privacy Policy

**App:** Everything (the "App")
**Last updated:** 11 October 2026
**Contact:** christianshemkai045@gmail.com

Everything is a local marketplace for Arusha, Tanzania. It connects customers with shops, service providers (fundis) and delivery riders. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. We wrote it to be read, so it is short and plain.

## 1. The most important points

- We do **not** handle any money. Payments happen directly between you and the shop or fundi (cash, mobile money, etc.). We never see or store your payment details.
- We collect only what the App needs to work.
- We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.
- You can ask us to delete your account and your data at any time (see section 7).

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us**
- **Account details:** your email address and password (the password is stored securely by our sign-in provider; we cannot read it), your name, phone number, area, and preferred language.
- **If you run a business or work as a fundi:** the business name, description, category, address notes, phone number, opening hours, payment methods and account numbers you choose to show to customers, items or services you list, and photos (logo, cover, product photos).
- **If you deliver as a rider:** your vehicle type and plate number (optional), and your online/offline status.
- **Orders and service requests:** what you ordered or requested, quantities, notes, delivery address or location pin, the status of each order or job, and photos you attach to a service request.
- **Reviews and ratings** you write after an order or job is completed.
- **Messages to support** and any reply you receive.

**Information collected from your device**
- **Location (only with your permission).** We use your precise location when you ask for places near you, when you choose to share your location as a delivery pin, and when a shop owner saves the shop's location. We do not track your location in the background.
- **Photos (only with your permission).** We access your photo library or camera only when you choose to upload a photo.
- **Push notification token.** If you allow notifications, we store a token for your device so we can send order and job updates.
- **Searches.** We keep what people search for in the App (and whether it found results) to improve search and to learn which shops or services are missing.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and run your account, and to sign you in.
- To show shops and fundis near you and to match your searches.
- To process orders and service requests, and to let shops, fundis and riders do their part.
- To send you notifications about your orders, jobs, deliveries and support replies.
- To show reviews, which unlock only after you confirm that an order or job was completed.
- To investigate reports and problems, keep the App safe, and prevent misuse.
- To understand overall use (for example how many orders were made) and to improve the App.

## 4. Who can see your information

- **Shops and fundis** you order from or request a job from can see your name, phone number, what you ordered or requested, and (for delivery or a job at your address) your delivery address or location pin.
- **Delivery riders** assigned to your order can see the pickup place, your name, phone number, and delivery address or pin for that order.
- **Customers** can see a business's public details (name, photos, area, phone, opening hours, payment methods, prices, ratings). If you leave a review, your first name or name is shown with it.
- **Our administrators** can see account, order and report information when needed to resolve problems, answer support messages, or enforce our rules.
- **Other people cannot see your email address, password, or your other orders.**

## 5. Service providers we use

We use trusted companies to run the App. They process information only to provide their service to us:
- **Supabase** – database, sign-in, file storage and messaging for the App.
- **Expo (push notification service)** – to deliver push notifications to your phone.
- **Apple and Google** – app stores, and the notification systems of your phone.
- **An email delivery service** – to send sign-in or password reset emails.
- **Map apps** – when you tap a map button, your phone opens its own map app (such as Google Maps) with an address or location. That app has its own privacy policy.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. Order, job and review records may be kept after deletion in a form that no longer identifies you, where needed to keep a business's history and ratings accurate, to resolve disputes, or to meet legal duties. Search records are kept only to improve search. If you delete your account, we remove or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable time.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can:
- Look at and change your name, phone, area and language in the App (Profile).
- Turn off location, camera/photos or notifications at any time in your phone's settings. Some features will then stop working.
- Ask us to **delete your account and personal data** – use the "Delete account" option in the App (Profile), or email us at [CONTACT EMAIL].
- Ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.

If you are in Tanzania, you have rights under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, and you may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

## 8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, access to data is limited by account (you can only see what you are meant to see), and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

## 9. Children

The App is not meant for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the App. Using the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
